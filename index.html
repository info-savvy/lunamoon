The ship

The only one of her kind in the Indian Ocean.

She has piloted ships in the English Channel, fished the Baltic, sat out a war on the seabed and led tall ship fleets across Europe.

Today she sails the unspoilt islands of Madagascar's northwest, reaching corners of the coast that very few visitors ever see, with a crew who will happily help you relax, explore, wine and dine, get sporty or (optionally) even put the guests of your choice to work on the sails!

Why Luna Moon

More ship, more space, more sailing.

Room for everyone

Wide teak decks, a big saloon and space for up to 11 guests. Most charter catamarans around Nosy Be take six to eight.

Real sailing

Take the helm, haul on a halyard or just watch the canvas go up. Join in as much or as little as you like.

Looked after

A full crew runs the ship, the galley and the plans, so your group can switch off for the week.

The experience

Every day is different.

Lunch on deck, a dive off the bow, a village market, a lesson at the helm.

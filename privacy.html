Privacy policy

Template for client review · last updated [launch date]

Madagascar Travel and Tours Ltd (Co. Reg. 248717), operator of Luna Moon, respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through lunamoon.org, why we collect it and your rights. It is written to meet South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What we collect

  • Details you send us: your name, email, phone number, travel dates, group size and any message.
  • Information about your visit: pages viewed, device and browser type, and approximate location, collected through cookies if you allow them.

How we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and plan and manage your charter.
  • To share your booking details with our sister company, The Madagascar Collection, only if you ask us to arrange land travel.
  • To understand how the website is used so we can improve it, with your consent.

We do not sell your information. We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to plan your trip and meet our legal obligations.

Cookies

Essential cookies make the site work. Analytics cookies (Google Analytics) help us see how visitors use the site, and we only set them if you accept them in the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time by clearing cookies in your browser.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent. Contact us at charter@lunamoon.org. You may also complain to South Africa's Information Regulator or, if you are in the EU, your local data protection authority.

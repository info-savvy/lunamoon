Why northern Madagascar

The ocean is the destination.

Reef life, island diving and the chance of meeting marine wildlife give northern Madagascar its own character. Around Nosy Be, the coastline and outlying islands make a striking setting for a small-group expedition.

This isn't a fixed resort route. Days are planned around the conditions at sea and the best dive sites on offer.

More than a dive trip

Islands, ocean and the freedom of sail.

Island landscapes

Coastlines, empty beaches and quiet anchorages with space to explore between dives.

Indian Ocean

Warm water, coral reefs, reef fish, turtles and rays, with seasonal visits from bigger animals.

Expedition spirit

A flexible route, guided by the weather and the sea, on a ship with more than a century of sailing behind her.

When to go

A season worth exploring.

Reef fish, turtles and rays are year-round. Humpback whales and whale sharks are seasonal, and sightings are never guaranteed.

May – October

The dry season

Generally drier, settled weather around Nosy Be.

September – October

Peak dive window

A strong time for diving and seasonal wildlife.

Mid-September – December

Whale shark season

Whale sharks visit the waters around Nosy Be at this time of year.

Your expedition

A week shaped by the sea.

No two dive weeks are the same. The route follows the weather, the sea and the best chances for diving, sailing and exploring.

Sample structure only. Final dive sites, number of dives, certification requirements and activities are confirmed with the operator.

  1. Day 1

    Arrive in Nosy Be, welcome on board and sail away.

  2. Days 2–3

    Coastal passages and planned dive days.

  3. Days 4–5

    Explore the islands and remote anchorages.

  4. Day 6

    A final planned dive day, then sunset at sea.

  5. Day 7

    Sailing and non-diving activities.

  6. Day 8

    Disembark in Nosy Be and fly home.

Who it's for

Dive clubs and private groups.

  • Dive clubs looking for a new destination
  • Private groups who want the whole boat
  • Experienced divers after something more adventurous than a standard liveaboard
  • Mixed groups with non-diving partners and friends
The vessel

Not an ordinary liveaboard.

Luna Moon is a 1910 Dutch topsail schooner with a remarkable sailing history, fully refitted in South Africa. Expect a small-group atmosphere, long days on deck and shared meals with the crew.

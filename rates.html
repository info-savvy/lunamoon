When to sail

Season

Nosy Be's sailing season runs from April to December, outside the cyclone months. The best weeks book early, especially July, August and school holidays.

Booking

How to book

  1. Send your dates and group size by WhatsApp or the enquiry form.
  2. We confirm availability and send a plan and quote.
  3. A deposit secures your dates. Full terms come with your quote.

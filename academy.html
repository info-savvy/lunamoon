Luna Moon Sailing Academy

Learn to sail on a classic tall ship.

Hands-on sail training for people starting a career at sea, and anyone who wants to learn properly.

Who it's for

Start a life at sea.

Our internships prepare people for work in superyachting, sailing and the marine industry. You live and work on board a 132 ft classic schooner, sailing the islands of the Indian Ocean.

We also run mile-building passages for sailors who need to log sea time.

Every trainee leaves with a reference letter from the captain.

What you'll learn
  • Superyacht introduction and safety equipment
  • Passage planning and sail handling
  • Rope work, dinghy handling and anchoring
  • VHF radio procedure
  • Working aloft safely in a bosun's chair
  • Basic engine servicing and fault-finding
  • Man overboard drills and watchkeeping
Included
  • Accommodation on board
  • All meals
  • Training and captain's reference
Not included
  • Flights and visas
  • Travel insurance
  • Personal kit: sleeping bag, head torch, sailing gloves, sun protection
Dates & fees

Programme dates, length and fees are sent on request.

Ask about the academy

Private charters on a 1910 Dutch topsail schooner, sailing the islands of northwest Madagascar from Nosy Be.
